# HuGPT-CS-Abstracts
A labeled, topic-balanced dataset of computer-science abstracts (human/ChatGPT) for textual deepfake detection and NLP research.

This repository contains two datasets of computer-science abstracts generated by large language models: one produced with ChatGPT-4o and the other with ChatGPT-5 (the latest version), alongside human-written abstracts.
This dataset can be introduced as the first comprehensive, up-to-date, and labeled collection in the field of abstracts of reputable scientific articles in computer science. It includes 2,048 article abstracts, of which 1,024 abstracts were written by human authors and their 1,024 corresponding abstracts were generated by ChatGPT models. Given the decisive role of data quality in the performance of deep learning models, the process of compiling these abstracts was carried out carefully and based on scientific principles.
First, the various subfields of computer science were extracted via the official website of the reputable publisher ACM. For each subfield, the top journals published by the leading publishers in this domain, including ACM, IEEE, Elsevier, and Springer, were selected. From among the articles published in these journals over the past four years, a set of articles was randomly selected and their abstracts were extracted as human samples. To produce the ChatGPT-generated versions of the corresponding abstracts, the title of the same article was given to the ChatGPT models (4o and 5), and using a single instruction, a scientific and structured abstract was generated. These ChatGPT-generated abstracts were added to the dataset alongside the human-written samples to enable direct comparison.


* Purpose of creating this dataset:

The main objective of creating this dataset is to provide a standard platform for research on detecting scientific texts generated by language models such as ChatGPT in the field of computer science. This collection enables the training and evaluation of machine text-detection systems and provides a suitable basis for analyzing the linguistic, content, and structural features of abstracts in human and ChatGPT texts.

* Components of this dataset:
Each entry in this dataset includes the following information:

--Article title

--Name of the publishing journal

--Name of the scientific publisher (IEEE, ACM, Elsevier, Springer)

--Type of abstract author (Human or ChatGPT)

--Year of publication

--Thematic category of the paper (a subfield of computer science)

--Abstract text

--Digital Object Identifier (DOI)

--Journal impact factor (Impact Factor)

* Proposed applications of this dataset:
This dataset can be used in the following areas:

-Training and evaluating machine learning models for detecting content generated by ChatGPT

-Analyzing writing style between human texts and ChatGPT

Subject classification of scientific texts based on abstracts

Qualitative evaluation of the structure and content of ChatGPT outputs in scientific writing
